# ARCA Adversarial Verification V0.1

Status: PROPOSED — Roadmap 13/16

A verifier attempts to **disconfirm or weaken** an investigative lead before it can advance to human review.

Checks include: provenance completeness, source independence, contradictory/refuting evidence, plausible non-wrongdoing explanations, temporal/relational overreach, stale/unavailable sources, and whether a red flag was mistaken for a finding.

Outcomes are procedural: `HOLD_FOR_MORE_EVIDENCE`, `ADVANCE_TO_HUMAN_REVIEW`, or `REJECT_LEAD`. They are not guilt findings.

Advancement requires no unresolved critical challenge and preserves all challenges/counter-evidence. Public ARCA stores verifier logic/tests; real challenges and case evidence remain private.
