# PNCP Blind Corpus Freeze V0.1

This gate converts completed PNCP deepening reports into a deterministic blind-evaluation corpus containing analytical inputs and provenance summaries, not detector answers.

Only completed, custody-gated PNCP reports are eligible. Detector findings and answer keys are excluded from the frozen corpus. Incomplete deepening is rejected unless an explicit incomplete-corpus option is supplied, and that state remains visible in the output.

The frozen corpus is evaluation material, not a finding about a procurement or entity. Human review and the ARCA evidence taxonomy remain authoritative.
