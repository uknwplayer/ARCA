# Code of Conduct

ARCA's public collaboration spaces are for technical work and evidence-based discussion.

Participants are expected to communicate professionally, critique ideas and code rather than people, avoid harassment or discrimination, respect privacy, and refrain from publishing credentials, personal data, or private investigation material.

Maintainers may remove disruptive or unsafe content and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project.

Security-sensitive disclosures should follow SECURITY.md rather than being posted publicly.
